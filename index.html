How It Works

From assignment to reconciled payment.

A single collection assignment moves through four stages before it's closed out.

Stage 1

Assign

A loan account with a due amount is routed to a field officer as a collection assignment.

Stage 2

Visit

The collector visits the borrower, logs the visit, and records the outcome — paid, partial, or promise to pay.

Stage 3

Reconcile

Payments collected in the field are matched against the borrower's outstanding balance automatically.

Stage 4

Report

Supervisors review collector activity and overdue accounts on the management dashboard.
